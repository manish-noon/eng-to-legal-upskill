## Treaty and local-law checkpoints

- UNIDROIT, [Convention on International Interests in Mobile Equipment](https://www.unidroit.org/instruments/security-interests/cape-town-convention/), adopted 2001. Articles 2–4 and 7 cover the interest, connecting factors and constitution. Articles 16, 19–22 and 29 cover registration, searching and priority. Articles 39–40 address certain non-consensual claims.
- UNIDROIT, [Aircraft Protocol](https://www.unidroit.org/instruments/security-interests/aircraft-protocol/), adopted 2001. Articles I, III–V and VII address eligible objects, sales, coverage and identification. Articles IX, XI, XIII and XIV address remedies, insolvency, IDERA, sales and engine priority. Conditions attach to particular remedies and elections. Check the transaction date, state declarations, relevant local law and operating facts.
- UNIDROIT depositary, [UAE entry in the list of states parties](https://www.unidroit.org/instruments/security-interests/aircraft-protocol/states-parties/) and declarations under [Article XXX(1)](https://www.unidroit.org/instruments/security-interests/aircraft-protocol/depositary/declarations-deposited-under-the-protocol-to-the-cape-town-convention-on-international-interests-in-mobile-equipment-on-matters-specific-to-aircraft-equipment-arranged-by-article/article-xxx1/) and [Article XXX(3)](https://www.unidroit.org/instruments/security-interests/aircraft-protocol/depositary/declarations-deposited-under-the-protocol-to-the-cape-town-convention-on-international-interests-in-mobile-equipment-on-matters-specific-to-aircraft-equipment-arranged-by-article/article-xxx3/). The UAE elected Article XIII and Alternative A with a 60-calendar-day waiting period. Article XI depends on the primary insolvency jurisdiction. The waiting period alone predicts neither repossession nor a flight date.
- UAE Legislation Portal, [Cabinet Resolution 52 of 2017, Executive Regulations on VAT, article 34(1)](https://tax.gov.ae/datafolder/Files/Legislation/Executive-Regulation-of-Federal-Decree-Law-No-08-of-2017-Publish-18-09-2025.pdf). The specified commercial-transport design or adaptation test and recreation/pleasure/sport exclusion matter for supply and import of aircraft. Assess each supply, location, facts and applicable version separately.
- UNCITRAL, [Model Law on International Commercial Arbitration, 1985, with 2006 amendments](https://uncitral.un.org/sites/default/files/media-documents/EN/Texts/UNCITRAL/Arbitration/07-86998_ebook.pdf), Articles 9, 20, 35–36. Article 20 distinguishes place of arbitration from meetings elsewhere. The Model Law is a legislative model. Verify the domestic law at the agreed seat and where relief is sought.

## Filed examples and financing structures

- The ordinary periodic-rent clock comparison comes from the [filed exhibit library](../../courses/aviation/docs/index.html), documents 11 §12.1, 13 and 14 Schedule 9(a), and 20 Schedule 9(a). Scope is ordinary nonpayment of periodic rent measured in business days from due date. Separate technical-transfer relief and other-payment clocks are excluded. The four recorded values are 3, 5, 3 and 5. Filing age, shared forms and disclosure selection preclude treating this as a current aviation market distribution.
- The [US Export-Import Bank aircraft export programme](https://www.exim.gov/solutions/loan-guarantee/transportation/aircraft-exports) describes eligible financing instruments. [JLPS's JOL and JOLCO description](https://www.jlps.co.jp/en/business/jol/) explains the Japanese investor and purchase-option structure. Transaction documents control the timing and conditions.
- Exhibit 21 §§19–21 distinguishes the airframe purchase option, residual payment and certification for registration. For the engines, read the separate engine rental economics. The local download for this exhibit used the public mirror identified in the source manifest.
