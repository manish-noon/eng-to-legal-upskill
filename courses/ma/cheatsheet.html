M&A cheatsheet: reading a deal and calling it

← Course · Handbook ·

Deal in 7 steps

Approach → NDA → LOI (non-binding; exclusivity binds) → due diligence → SPA negotiation → signing … conditions … closing → true-up, earn-out, claims.

Seller's leverage peaks before exclusivity. Buyer's grows through diligence.

Five kinds of promise

WarrantyStatement of fact. Breach → damages; prove loss; cut by disclosure and limits.
IndemnityPay if X happens. Pound for pound; outside basket, cap, disclosure.
CovenantPromise to do / not do.
ConditionMust happen before closing, or either side can walk at the long-stop date.
RepresentationUS: same as warranty. English: avoided (rescission risk).

Price

Equity = EV − debt − debt-like + cash ± (working capital − peg)

Completion accounts: true-up after closing; buyer-friendly; disputes. Locked box: fixed price off old accounts; no leakage; seller-friendly.

Earn-out: bridges valuation gap, breeds disputes. Escrow: price held back as security.

UAE debt-like: unfunded end-of-service gratuity.

Route every diligence finding

Known + countable→ price
Known + uncertain→ specific indemnity (+ escrow)
Fixable→ condition
Unknown→ warranty
Kills the rationale→ walk

Disclosed matters cannot found a warranty claim. Known problem ≠ warranty.

Claim pipeline (general warranties)

  1. Time limit: notified in time?
  2. De minimis: single claim big enough?
  3. Basket: total over threshold? Tipping = recover all. Deductible = excess only.
  4. Cap: maximum payable.
  5. Other reducers: recovered elsewhere, in the accounts, mitigation.

Bypass: fraud (everything); fundamental warranties (basket; cap = price); specific indemnities (own terms).

Example: price 76m, de minimis 76k, basket 760k, cap 15.2m. Claims 50k + 300k + 600k → counted 900k → tipping pays 900k; deductible pays 140k.

Split by insurance first. Never average the two. Cap often = escrow. Fundamental cap = 100%; fraud uncapped: these do not move.

W&I insurance

Insurer pays for unknown warranty breaches above the retention, up to the limit. Seller cap → nominal. Excludes known issues, price adjustments, undiligenced areas, and (Middle East) war and sanctions. Known risks still need indemnity or price.

Recourse test: who is the seller after closing, and where is the money?

Landing model

  1. Unit: % of price / months / category.
  2. Comparables: insurance → seller type → size → legal culture.
  3. Band P25 / median / P75, state n (≥ 4).
  4. Leverage: 8 factors, +1 / 0 / −1, sum, clip ±2.
  5. landing = median + |score|/2 × (edge − median)clamp between the openings.
  6. Say what would move it.

Leverage (+ buyer / − seller): bilateral / auction · seller must sell / can keep · no W&I / W&I · red flags / clean · sellers leave / stay · financial buyer / strategic need · buyer's / seller's market · clean close / approvals.

Kept risk → verdict

kept = Σ p × (loss − actually recoverable)effective price = price + kept + deal costs

vs value = standalone + synergies − integration.

≤ value, no triggerPursue
≤ value only with protectionsConditions (each with a price fallback)
> value, or trigger hitWalk / re-price

Triggers: uncovered known material risk · ruinous tail · approval cannot come in time · no money behind the protection · dishonest disclosure.

Cap above worst credible loss = worth zero. Deal-rationale risks → conditions, not indemnities. Ignore sunk costs. State the break-even.

UAE checks

  • Onshore / DIFC / ADGM: target's home, governing law and forum are three separate choices.
  • Merger control: AED 300m relevant UAE sales or 40% share; file ≥ 90 days before closing; suspensory; silence = rejection.
  • Mainland LLC transfer: notary + amended memorandum + licence.
  • Gratuity: 21 days/yr (first 5), 30 days/yr after, cap 2 years' pay.
  • Emiratisation; licence activities; Arabic originals; VAT / corporate tax history.
  • Onshore courts may adjust agreed sums. Ask, do not assert.

Reading an SPA fast

Order: parties → price definitions → conditions and termination → indemnification / limits → their definitions → rest.

Search: Deductible · Basket · Threshold · Cap · "shall not exceed" · survive · "exclusive remedy" · Knowledge · Fraud · "R&W Insurance" · Leakage · Escrow · "Material Adverse Effect" · "without giving effect to".

US → English: reps = warranties · Closing = completion · indemnification = warranty claims + limits · survival = time limit · RWI = W&I · outside date = long-stop.

AI: five errors to check

Wrong mechanism · tipping / deductible misread · wrong number · invented "market" statistic · dropped carve-out. Every number needs a clause reference you have opened.

Say it like this

  • "Where does that usually land, and what moves it?"
  • "Is that market, or what one client got?"
  • "That's a known issue, so it wants an indemnity or a price chip, not a warranty."
  • "What's behind the indemnity: escrow or a guarantee?"
  • "The cap isn't where our risk is. I'd trade it for the condition."
  • "On expected terms we pay about X for something worth Y. It turns on these two points."
