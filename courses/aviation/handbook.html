Aircraft agreements handbook

The clause, the counterparty and the cash consequence. Keep this reference beside the agreement as you work through the issues list.

First pass through an unfamiliar agreement. Identify the complete signed set and any missing attachments. Pick one live question, find its operative verb, then read each incorporated definition and exception back into that sentence. Record who acts, what evidence counts, when the right expires and what happens on silence. Compare any redline with the last operative wording before describing a concession.

Module 01

Party and asset map

Draw separate arrows for title, possession, rent, purchase price and security. Identify the company on each end and any limits on its recourse. Pair aircraft and engine serial numbers with the promised records.

Instructions for the next draft

Ask for the owner’s signature, the lender’s conditional release and the operator’s acceptance authority. A brand painted on an aircraft proves none of these.

Module 02

The price bridge

Begin with the dated configuration and component price bases. Trace the escalation index, reference month, lag, caps, floors and substitutions. Check which options escalate and what happens after delay.

Instructions for the next draft

Separate cash credits from credits usable only for spares or services. Value the latter using expected eligible spend before expiry. Obtain the signed side letter and identify which document controls.

Module 03

Deposits and delivery

List the instalment dates, amount, beneficiary, due condition, refund event and guarantee. Credit the cumulative payments to final price. Map technical acceptance separately from legal delivery.

Instructions for the next draft

Set a release sequence for residual payment, insurance, signed acceptance, title and risk. Require a closing record that shows who held what at the instant of release.

Module 04

Delay, warranty and support

Compare the applicable date to actual tender. Allocate each day to cause, notice, excused periods and contractual grace. Apply daily relief, cap, exclusivity and long-stop rights in that order.

Instructions for the next draft

For each defect identify the warrantor, remedy, claim deadline and excluded expense. An expiring support credit and free parts can leave significant downtime and installation cost.

Module 05

Buying used

Read the letter of intent for binding provisions, inspection access and deposit release. Test actual delivery condition against the agreed baseline and traceable records. Separate physical condition, title warranties and intervening damage.

Instructions for the next draft

Price the defect or require cure before closing. For an as-is airframe verify engines are included and identify explicit carve-outs. Tie the delivery receipt and dated bills of sale to escrow instructions.

Module 05b

Reading the closing set

A technical election, delivery receipt and escrow direction do different jobs. Check who signed each and which discrepancies survive acceptance. Put notice, repair, bank and long-stop clocks on one calendar. Track the time zone and the consequence of silence.

Instructions for the next draft

If the seller wants to close with work open, agree the cure standard, evidence, funded protection and decision date. Confirm the lender and escrow agent can act on the signed instructions.

Module 06

Rent and security

Find the contractual event that starts rent, advance or arrears timing, currency and payment location. Price the cost of immobilised collateral and any refund risk. Separate a lessor’s right of draw from a lessee’s right to apply collateral.

Instructions for the next draft

For unconditional rent, budget the grounding period and identify a separate recovery. Obtain direct promises from parties able to interfere with possession.

Module 07

Reserves and return

Build a ledger by component and event. Enter opening balance, usage contributions, eligible invoice, limits and reimbursement timing. Test the return standard on hours, cycles, calendar life and records.

Instructions for the next draft

Compare the physical cure with any agreed cash alternative. A maintenance reserve already funded is not a second loss. Model expiry, reimbursement restrictions and an overrun separately.

Module 07b

Running the aircraft under lease

Identify the proposed operator, who supplies the crew and who retains operational control. Follow the possession restriction into the relevant exception and its conditions. Check the required consents and evidence for the actual flight or maintenance event.

Instructions for the next draft

Name the exact operation the lessor may approve, what reports it will receive and who bears continuing liability. A technical clearance and a commercial permission each have their own issuer.

Module 08

Casualty and default

Separate hull value, liability limits and indemnity scope. Identify who receives proceeds, who still owes rent after a loss and when agreed value falls due. Track the period between payment and collection.

Instructions for the next draft

For default, distinguish a breach from an event of default and an elected remedy. Check notice and cure, insolvency restrictions, possession, records, local procedure and practical export.

Module 08b

Reading a marked lease

Put the original clause, tracked edit, reviewer’s question and later text side by side. Follow the same defined term into the schedule. A right can survive in narrower form, move to another document or remain open. An accepted-clean reading copy establishes the proposed reading.

Instructions for the next draft

Write down the permission gained, the condition attached and the evidence still missing. The party allowed to submit a warranty claim, the party entitled to the eventual cash and the party funding the repair can be different.

Module 09

Financing structures

Follow debt service through trustee, lender, owner and airline. In a finance lease map payment obligations and residual or option exposure. Export credit supports a defined beneficiary. A Japanese operating lease with call option introduces an exercise decision and local investor economics.

Instructions for the next draft

Compare the exercise price with the net cost of returning the aircraft on the option date. Stress the funding, tax representation and indemnity assumptions before a term sheet becomes a commitment.

Module 10

Priority and export

Identify the object and treaty connecting facts, declarations and any competing interest. National registration, international registration and an IDERA are different records. Check each against the serial numbers.

Instructions for the next draft

Turn the remedy into a sequence with owners. Obtain priority searches and consents, check insolvency and procedural conditions, secure records, insurance, airworthiness and export clearances.

Module 11

Map supply, delivery, import, rent and redelivery to locations, parties and dates. Trace each tax to its legal payer and contractual bearer. Test withholding, exceptions to gross-up and mitigation.

Module 12

A defensible forecast

Start with the disputed field and a defensible comparable set. Count distinct observations, normalise units and event triggers, then report quartiles. Score alternatives, slot scarcity, timing, financing and control of the draft from the client’s side.

Instructions for the next draft

State who gains as the number rises. Move toward the relevant edge, bound by the asks, name overrides and describe which new fact could change your prediction.

Module 13

The committee instruction

Price ordinary costs and probability-weighted losses on a common date, net only of collectible recovery. Avoid double-counting correlated states and deposits. Check tail loss and peak liquidity against the mandate.

Instructions for the next draft

Give a decision on the current paper. Specify the break-even concession and the clause that must change. Assign verification to a named role. Escalate non-waivable title and enforcement gates separately.
