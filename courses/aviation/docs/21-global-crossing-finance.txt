LEASE AGREEMENT (MSN 5999), dated as of August 24, 2026 (this “Lease Agreement”), between Bank of Utah, not in its individual capacity but solely as owner trustee, a corporation organized under the laws of the State of Utah and having its principal place of business at 50 South 200 East, Suite 110, Salt Lake City, Utah, 84111, USA (“Lessor”), and Global Crossing Airlines, Inc., a company organized and existing under the laws of the State of Delaware, having its principal place of business at 4200 NW 36thStreet, Miami International Airport, Building 5A, 4thFloor, Miami, FL, USA, 33161 (“Lessee”).

RECITAL:

Lessee desires, upon the terms and conditions hereof, and of the other Operative Documents, to lease the Aircraft from Lessor, and Lessor desires, upon the terms and conditions hereof and of the other Operative Documents, to lease the Aircraft to Lessee.

AGREEMENT:

In consideration of the foregoing premise, and for other good and valuable consideration the adequacy and receipt of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties hereto agree as follows:

1. Definitions; Construction and Interpretation
1.1 Definitions

The capitalized terms used in this Lease Agreement will have the respective meanings set forth in Part I of Schedule 1.

1.2 Construction and Interpretation

The rules of construction and interpretation for this Lease Agreement and each other Operative Document are set forth in Part II of Schedule 1.

2. Lease of Aircraft
2.1 Delivery

Lessor agrees to deliver the Aircraft to Lessee at the Delivery Location and to lease the Aircraft to Lessee, and Lessee agrees to accept the Aircraft at the Delivery Location and to lease the Aircraft from Lessor, in each case, subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions precedent set out in Schedule 7 and Schedule 8 and otherwise subject to the terms and conditions of the Operative Documents. Subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the conditions precedent set out in Schedule 8 and otherwise subject to the terms and conditions of the Operative Documents, Lessee will execute and deliver the Acceptance Certificate to Lessor on the Delivery Date. The delivery procedures are more fully set out in Schedule 3.

2.2 Lease Term

Lessee will lease the Airframe pursuant hereto for the Lease Term-Airframe. Lessee will lease each Engine pursuant hereto for the applicable Lease Term-Engine.

2.3 Return

Lessee will return each Engine to Lessor at the Return Location, and Lessor agrees to accept each Engine at the Return Location from Lessee, in each case, on the last day of the Lease Term and in compliance with the Return Condition, subject to the terms and conditions of the Operative Documents. Lessor will execute and deliver the Return Acceptance Certificate to Lessee at the Return. The return procedures are more fully set out in Schedule 4.

3. Rent; Payments
3.1 Rent

3.1.1 Airframe Rent – Financing

Lessee will pay rent for the Airframe to Lessor on each Rent Payment Date in an amount equal to the Rent—Financing Amount.

3.1.2 Engine Rent—Periodic

Lessee will pay periodic rent for each Engine to Lessor on each Rent Payment Date in an amount equal to the Rent—Periodic Amount.

3.2 Rent—Supplemental

Lessee will pay promptly to Lessor, or to whosoever is entitled thereto, any and all Rent—Supplemental when and as the same becomes due and owing.

3.3 Airframe Balloon Payment.

Lessee will pay the Balloon Payment to Lessor on the Lease Term Expiry Date – Airframe.

3.4 Payments in General

3.4.1 Timing and Place of Payment

All payments of Rent will be made directly by Lessee in Dollars by wire transfer of immediately available funds on the required date of payment, for receipt on such date and with value on such date, (1) if due to Lessor, to the account for Lessor specified in Schedule 5 or to such other account as Lessor will otherwise designate by not less than 5 Business Days’ prior written notice to Lessee and (2) if due to any other Person in accordance with the terms of this Lease Agreement or any other Operative Document, to such account as such Person will designate by not less than 5 Business Days’ prior written notice to Lessee. Any amounts owing by Lessor to Lessee under any Operative Document will be paid by wire transfer of immediately available Dollars to Lessee’s account specified in Schedule 5, or to such other account as Lessee will otherwise designate by not less than 5 Business Days’ prior written notice to Lessor, provided that if Lessee claims against Lessor any amount in a currency other than Dollars, Lessor may, at its option, pay such amount either in the currency in which it is claimed or in Dollars, and in either case Lessor may convert such amount into Dollars at the market rate of exchange available to Lessor or Servicer in London or New York on the date that the relevant payment is made. If Lessee receives a notice or other communication in which Lessor or any such Person (or someone purporting to be Lessor or such other Person) designates an account for payment (other than an account specified in Schedule 5), then prior to making any payment to such account, Lessee will verify the authenticity of such designation by telephone or email contact with Lessor using the contact details specified in Schedule 5. If Lessor receives a notice or other communication which Lessee (or someone purporting to be Lessee) designates an account for payment (other than an account specified in Schedule 5), then prior to making any payment to such account, Lessor will verify the authenticity of such designation by telephone contact with Lessee using the contact details specified in Schedule 5.

The portion of the Security Deposit-Airframe that has not previously been applied or retained as provided in any Operative Document will be applied against the payment of the Balloon Payment by Lessee.

(1) Forthwith pay to the recipient such additional amount so that the net amount received by such recipient after the deduction or withholding will equal the full amount which would have been received by it had no such deduction or withholding been made.

(2) Pay to the relevant taxing authorities within the period for payment permitted by applicable Law the full amount of the deduction or withholding (including, but without prejudice to the generality of the foregoing, the full amount of any deduction or withholding from any additional amount paid pursuant to this Section 3).

(3) Furnish to such recipient, within the period for payment permitted by applicable Law, an official receipt of the relevant taxation or other authorities involved for all amounts deducted or withheld as aforesaid or, if no such receipt is issued, a certificate of deduction or equivalent evidence thereof.
