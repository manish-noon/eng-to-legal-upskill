Open exhibit 01 →

ProvisionFunctionQuestion for the issues list
0Definitions and interpretationWhat is included in Aircraft, Airframe and Warranted Part? Follow each defined price to its operative clause.
1–2Aircraft and specificationWhich units are ordered and which configuration is binding? Distinguish manufacturer changes from buyer changes and find the signed change form.
3–5Price, revision and paymentsReconcile base periods, airframe and propulsion price revision, pre-delivery payments and balance. Carry any redactions through the model.
6–7Inspection and certificationWho can inspect during production and which certificates must the seller obtain? Regulatory certification and compliance with the buyer’s specification answer different questions.
8–9Technical acceptance and deliveryMap attendance, possible deemed completion, signed acceptance, payment and delivery of the bill of sale. Mark title and casualty risk on the timeline.
10–11DelaySeparate excusable causes from other delay. Find the notice, grace, rate, cap, outside date and consequence of electing termination.
12Warranties and service lifeWhich defect qualifies, whose part is covered and what performance is owed? Price the labour and lost use the remedy leaves behind.
13Intellectual property indemnityIf use of the aircraft attracts a patent or copyright claim, who controls the response and what use or modification falls outside protection?
14–17Data, software, representatives, training and suppliersList the manuals, access rights, people and training needed for entry into service. Identify who provides each and when support expires.
18Buyer-furnished equipmentWho selects, delivers and integrates buyer-supplied items? Trace a late or defective component to the resulting delay and expense.
19Indemnification and insuranceIdentify the protected party, event and exceptions. Match each assumed liability to the responding policy and limits.
20TerminationFor each trigger record who may elect, the notice and time limit, what happens to advances, and which obligations survive.
21–22Transfer and miscellaneousCan the buyer substitute a financing vehicle or transfer claims? Check consents, notices, governing law and which text controls.
Exhibits and letter agreementsOperational detailFollow the change, acceptance and bill-of-sale forms and price formula. Check later modifications to base provisions before valuing a remedy.

5–6Representations and ongoing covenantsIdentify which facts must be true when made and which financial statements or other evidence must be delivered later.
7–8Ownership, filings and possessionWho maintains title protection and filings? Which subleases or changes of possession need consent and who remains liable?
9IndemnitiesMap claimant, claim, exception, demand and defence. Identify any exposure that survives return or exceeds available coverage.
10–11Loss and insuranceTrace aircraft loss, engine-only loss and requisition separately. Match insurance proceeds to agreed value, continuing rent and replacement duties.
12–13Default and remediesFind the rent clock and any transfer-error exception. Then read the remedy, election, required notice and local-law overlay.
14–15Transfer and set-offTest an owner-side transfer for non-disturbance and payment instructions. Check whether the airline can deduct a separate claim from rent.
16–18Further assurances, forum and miscellaneousIdentify later cooperation duties and their cost. Locate the court, service arrangements, notice addresses and amendment process.
Schedules 2, 4 and 5Operation, return and taxPrice the maintenance obligation, the return test and any tax gross-up separately. Schedule 3 is marked intentionally omitted.
Exhibits A–FEvidence and undertakingsCheck the actual acceptance, insurance certificate, broker letter, letter of credit, guarantee and Eurocontrol letter against the promised forms.
