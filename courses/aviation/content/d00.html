Baseline diagnostic

Take this cold. Missed items point to the relevant module. This diagnostic has no pass mark.

Purchase desk

A bank finances an aircraft owned by a special-purpose company and flown by an airline. Who can promise to transfer the aircraft free of that bank’s mortgage?

  • The owner must procure the release and convey title
  • The pilot can consent to the transfer
  • The airline can release the bank’s security by returning possession

Possession, title and security belong on separate lines of the party map. Trace authority to the owner and the creditor.

A cabin option is agreed by email. The signed specification excludes it. Which evidence would give the buyer its best contractual claim to this configuration?

  • An authorised change order incorporated under the agreement
  • A sales slide showing the same cabin
  • A simulator image with the proposed layout

Check the document hierarchy and change process before treating a commercial promise as binding.

A hypothetical purchase escalates only the $4m airframe component by 3%. What is the dollar increase?

$4,000,000 × 0.03 = $120,000. Apply the rate to the subject component.

At delivery the final price is $18m. Pre-delivery payments of $3.4m must be credited. Ignoring fees, how many dollars remain payable?

The deposits are instalments. $18m less $3.4m leaves $14.6m.

A delay is called excusable. What do you need before treating the delayed days as outside the damages calculation?

  • Evidence of the defined cause, its impact and compliance with the relief conditions
  • The date on the supplier’s press release
  • An assertion that late delivery is beyond the sales team’s control

Test the event against the wording, notice and causal link. The label alone establishes little.

A seller offers an as-is aircraft. An engine’s life records are incomplete. Which change directly addresses the buyer’s exposure?

  • Make traceable records a delivery condition with a defined rejection or adjustment right
  • Ask for an extra exterior photograph
  • Deliver the bill of sale one hour earlier

The records can determine permitted use and future value even if the engine runs.

Lease desk

The aircraft is grounded, and the lease makes base rent unconditional after acceptance. What should the operator put in its cash forecast?

  • Continuing rent while pursuing any separate repair or warranty claim
  • Zero rent until repaired
  • Only the security deposit

Use the actual payment covenant and any express exception. A claim against someone else does not automatically suspend rent.

A synthetic lease charges $160 per engine hour for one named engine. It ran for 210 hours this month. What is the reserve payment in dollars?

210 × $160 = $33,600. Do not multiply by the aircraft’s engine count.

The operator paid its liability premium but territorial cover excludes a proposed destination. What closes the gap?

  • Obtain the required policy endorsement before operating there
  • Increase the contract indemnity alone
  • File the certificate with the national registry

An indemnity allocates cost between parties. It cannot amend the insurer’s undertaking.

A financing proposal gives the airline a purchase option. Which fact most directly affects whether buying will make economic sense?

  • Exercise price versus expected aircraft value and return costs at the exercise date
  • The name of the trustee
  • The font used in the rental schedule

Compare exercise and return on a common date, including conditions and transaction costs.

Cross-border closing

The airline has a certificate of national registration. Which additional inquiry addresses competing international interests?

  • Search the International Registry for each relevant identifiable object
  • Accept the nationality mark as proof of first priority
  • Ask the insurer for a premium refund

National registration and the treaty priority search answer different questions.

The draft selects English law. Delivery and import occur elsewhere. Which fact still needs investigation?

  • The taxes and customs procedures linked to the physical transaction
  • Whether the contract can be written in English
  • Whether the parties can use one PDF

A contractual choice of law does not set the place of supply or decide customs obligations.

Investment committee

Only two comparables disclose the same rent default clock. What reaches the credit committee?

  • Both observations and the limits on inference, with a defensible wider filter if available
  • A market median with no sample size
  • An average including redacted periods entered as zero

The unknown periods cannot supply numerical observations.

A shortfall costs $500,000 if it occurs, with $200,000 collectible in that state and a 10% chance of occurrence. What is expected kept risk in dollars?

0.10 × ($500,000 − $200,000) = $30,000.

The economics fit but the board requires a lien release that cannot be obtained before closing. The mandate cannot be waived. What is the call?

  • Walk on the offered closing
  • Sign and record an expected loss of zero
  • Sign if the seller repeats the purchase price

An explicit unresolved veto controls even with positive expected headroom.
