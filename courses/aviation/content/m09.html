Financing structures

Trace who finances the aircraft, who can buy it, and who absorbs a shortfall if the airline exits early. You can compare an operating lease, a finance lease and two financing structures without guessing from their labels.

Follow the terminal obligation

A finance lease can give an airline possession while arranging for its payments to repay the capital advanced to acquire an aircraft and the cost of that funding. The lessor can retain legal title pending fulfilment of the terms. The economic bargain may put much of the value risk on the airline through rent, a terminal purchase obligation or a stipulated termination payment. Compare an operating lease that expects the aircraft back for another placement. Its owner is exposed to what the next operator will pay and therefore cares about the condition, records and remaining life delivered at return.

Treat “finance lease” here as a transaction description. Accounting, tax, insolvency and property law can each classify the same documents under their own tests. A presentation describing an aircraft as owned, financed or leased may be using one of those lenses. Ask which conclusion matters to your decision and obtain advice in the relevant jurisdiction. For commercial comparison you can begin before that advice arrives by constructing the cash flows on delivery, during operation, on planned expiry and following early termination.

In a filed finance lease, the parties certify that the lessor took legal title by bill of sale. They also certify that failure to exercise the purchase option obliges the lessee to pay a residual value or termination sum at least as large as the option price. Read section 21(c) before describing non-exercise as a way to save the exercise price. Then ask what property or use the airline receives on each path. Aircraft lease, section 21, Finance Lease Certification.

The revealing question is what happens after the last regular rental. Does title pass automatically, does a party have a right to buy, is a final large payment mandatory, or must the aircraft be returned in a specified condition? Read the clause and all conditions to transfer. A purchase option can require timely notice, payment of a fixed price and discharge of every other outstanding obligation. Title may sit with an owner trustee, who holds it under a trust arrangement and needs instructions. Identify who executes the bill of sale and who releases the lender's security.

Rebuild one financing

Draw an illustrative owner company with a single aircraft. Equity means the owner's invested capital at risk after senior creditors are paid. Senior debt is borrowing with the agreed first claim on the specified repayment sources ahead of equity. On delivery, the bank advances USD 18 million and investors contribute USD 6 million. The owner pays the manufacturer USD 24 million and leases the aircraft to the operator. During the term the airline pays rent to a designated account. A payment waterfall specifies the order in which money in that account pays expenses, debt service and amounts available to the investors.

The security package may include a mortgage over the aircraft, assignment of rentals and rights under insurance. An assignment redirects rights to another person. Read the notice delivered to the airline and any acknowledgement it signs. Identify whether payment to the specified account discharges the airline and who can change account details. A bank taking security over lease rights may reserve approval over lease amendments or early termination. A bargain made with the owner's commercial team can still stall if the bank's consent is a condition.

Separate the loan from the lease. A non-recourse loan to an owner vehicle can limit a lender's rights against the owner's shareholders to agreed assets, cash flows and exceptions. It does not follow that the airline has no payment obligations. The lender may rely heavily on the lease precisely because the owner's other resources are limited. Ask for the relevant guarantee or indemnity if a counterparty claims another entity will stand behind it. The existence of powerful investors in the ownership chain gives the airline no automatic claim on their balance sheets.

An aircraft costs USD 36 million. The owner receives USD 27 million of debt at delivery. Assume no fees, other sources or unpaid price. How many dollars of equity are required?

Price minus debt is USD 9 million.

A lender takes an assignment of rent. What should the airline seek in the written directions?

  • An immediate reduction of rent
  • Confirmation that payment to the designated account discharges its rent obligation
  • An automatic transfer of title

The direction changes the collection path. Discharge language protects against paying the same instalment twice.

Read-along · Who owes the deposit after a sale? In this hypothetical financing, the airline lodged a cash deposit with the original owner. A buyer sends new rent instructions. The lease permits a sale if the buyer takes on the owner's duties and meets a financial test, either itself or through a guarantor. Follow the cash in both directions. The payment notice identifies who collects next month's rent. The signed transfer agreement should identify who must refund the deposit at expiry and from what date.

Suppose the buyer shows you its parent company's accounts. Those accounts tell you about the parent. If the buyer relies on a guarantee to meet the test, ask for the signed guarantee, check that it covers the refund and establish when it takes effect. Before acknowledging release of the seller, reconcile the deposit balance with both parties. An unpaid refund can leave the airline exposed even after every rental has reached the correct account.

Price the end and the early exit

A balloon is a substantial final payment left after regular instalments. An option strike is the price payable if a purchase option is properly exercised. One can be obligatory while the other depends on election. A stipulated loss value schedule sets the amount due following specified events and may decline over time. Keep the three columns separate. A value in the casualty schedule need not be the airline's voluntary prepayment price. A request for early exit is also a request to identify whether the airline has any contractual exit right at all.

In a teaching comparison, a fleet planner discounts all future payments to the same date and currency. Under a purchase path the present value of future rent is USD 12 million, the option strike is USD 5 million on the same present-value basis and the aircraft retained at exit is worth USD 4 million in today's money. The simplified net acquisition-and-use cost is USD 13 million. In a return path use its own rent and return expenses, with no residual credit to the airline. If ownership is mandatory, describe that as an obligation even where the arithmetic resembles an option exercise.

Early settlement can include outstanding principal, accrued return, break costs and fees. Break costs are defined losses from ending a funding or hedging arrangement early. Require a calculation statement and evidence for each input. Ask whether gains from unwinding the same position are credited, which discount rate applies, whether notice is irrevocable before the price is fixed and whether the airline can verify quotations. A fixed rental can still sit alongside a volatile early exit price. A route network that may contract before lease expiry puts extra value on a predictable exit.

The loss-value schedule is published but the airline wants to return a perfectly serviceable aircraft early. What should you do first?

  • Treat the next loss value as the buyout price
  • Find a voluntary termination right and its pricing method
  • Stop paying rent when the airline offers the keys

A casualty formula assigns consequences to a different trigger. It grants no right to surrender early by itself.

What export credit support changes

An export credit agency, or ECA, supports eligible exports under its programme. The US Export-Import Bank describes direct loans, guarantees and insurance among its aircraft export tools. For a guaranteed loan, distinguish the bank advancing cash, the party borrowing, the agency assuming specified credit exposure to the lender, and the operator undertaking obligations under its lease. The agency's support can improve the availability of funding. It does not insure the airline against weak passenger demand or excuse rent when a route closes. EXIM aircraft export financing programmes.

“a first priority mortgage on the financed aircraft”

EXIM identifies this as one possible element of the aircraft-backed security supporting its credit decision. For a term sheet, list the actual requirements on eligibility, documentary evidence, aircraft security, reporting and disbursement. A commitment subject to unmet conditions is weaker than money ready for delivery. If the manufacturer can tender before financing conditions clear, draw the gap between the delivery obligation and earliest drawdown. Agree who provides bridge funds, what happens to deposits and whether delivery can be deferred under the purchase agreement.

Ask for the exact support instrument, its beneficiary and its cost. For instance a premium charged upfront should enter the all-in cost at its actual payment date. If the premium is financed, include the resulting financing charges. Keep different countries' programmes separate and avoid treating a published description as an approval for this buyer. Look for a written commitment against this transaction. Put the expiry of a conditional offer, the delivery window and any change in export content side by side.

A lender has an ECA guarantee. The airline's route becomes uneconomic. Which obligation does that guarantee ordinarily address in the described structure?

  • Refund of the airline's operating losses
  • Covered credit risk owed to the lender under the instrument
  • Release of all airline lease payments

The protected party is the lender. An airline needs a separate contractual basis for relief.

Ask treasury to demonstrate one failed drawdown using the proposed timetable. Assume the owner has approval subject to evidence of a clean security position, but a release document is still in transit. Who has the ability to postpone acceptance, which party can extend the financing commitment and does interest accrue on emergency funding? The consequences turn on the connections between instruments. A lender's commercial assurance alone cannot move the manufacturer's delivery deadline.

Read both branches of a JOLCO

A Japanese operating lease with call option, known as a JOLCO, combines Japanese investor equity with debt and gives the lessee a purchase option under specified conditions. The structure can include several companies, a lease or sublease and a secured lender. Find the party holding legal title, the party holding each option and the parties bearing residual value if the option expires unused. The option has value because the lessee can compare the exercise economics with the alternative. Read the alternative before telling the airline it has a free exit. JP Lease Products & Services, JOL and JOLCO structures.

Build a calendar with the notice deadline, exercise date, strike, transfer costs and any conditions. One SEC-filed financing description sets the JOLCO purchase right on a fixed date before lease expiry. That detail matters. If the lessee misses the date, the remaining lease obligations and return exposure may persist. Work through both branches from the same decision date. Obtain legal and tax advice on the particular investors, transactions and jurisdictions before discounting the lease for an assumed tax benefit. A tax benefit for an investor does not automatically belong to the airline. Filed financing description, JOLCO facilities.

A non-recourse loan funds an owner vehicle. Which conclusion about the airline follows from that description alone?

  • It can stop rent after any owner default
  • It must guarantee every shareholder loss

Key terms

Finance lease
Lease structured to fund aircraft acquisition through contractual payment obligations.
Equity
Capital exposed to residual gains and losses after senior claims.
Senior debt
Borrowing ranking ahead of specified junior claims.
Waterfall
Contractual sequence for applying available cash.
Assignment
Transfer of rights such as the right to receive rent.
Non-recourse
Recovery limited to agreed assets or sources, subject to stated exceptions.
Balloon
Substantial final payment remaining after periodic instalments.
Break costs
Defined costs following early termination of funding or a hedge.
ECA
Export credit agency supporting eligible international trade.
JOLCO
Japanese operating lease with call option.
Strike
Price payable on valid exercise of a purchase option.
