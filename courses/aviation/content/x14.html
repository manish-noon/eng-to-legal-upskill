Purchase desk

A signed aircraft lease names a trustee solely in its trust capacity. The negotiator values a refund promise at face value based on the bank’s own balance sheet. What must be established first?

  • Whether recourse reaches the bank or is limited to trust assets
  • Whether the airline uses the bank’s currency
  • Whether the trustee signed electronically

Read the capacity and recourse wording. A signature by a well-capitalised institution can bind it only in a limited capacity.

A new purchase uses a $9m adjustable component and $2m of fixed options. Only the adjustable component increases by 4%. A fully usable $120,000 credit offsets delivery cash. What net amount is due in dollars if no instalments were paid?

$9m × 1.04 + $2m − $120,000 = $11.24m.

An OEM offers a $600,000 credit for future spares, expiring before the buyer will need any. Which fallback most directly restores economic value?

  • Permit application to the delivery balance or extend eligibility to committed purchases
  • Replace the word credit with rebate while retaining all restrictions
  • Record the full credit as cash in the closing statement

Eligibility, expiry and application determine how much can actually be used.

A buyer has paid $2m of deposits. The refund guarantee pays only 75% of those deposits upon a covered failure. In that state the seller estate pays nothing. What deposit principal in dollars stays exposed?

Uncovered principal is $2m × 25% = $500,000. A covered trigger still leaves the uninsured portion.

Of 28 late days, 8 are excused. Another 4 chargeable days fall in a non-accruing grace period. Damages are $12,000 per remaining day, capped at $175,000. What is payable in dollars?

16 days × $12,000 = $192,000 before the cap. The cap reduces payment to $175,000.

A replacement part is free, but the buyer must pay labour, freight and the cost of a grounded aircraft. Which conclusion belongs in the warranty memo?

  • Parts recovery leaves installation and interruption costs with the buyer on these facts
  • The warranty makes the buyer economically whole
  • The entire purchase price becomes recoverable

Model the remedy line by line. Parts coverage gives no automatic cash for labour or lost operations.

The seller signs a bill of sale into escrow. The aircraft suffers damage before release. The contract transfers risk only on release. Which instruction follows?

  • Stop release and apply the pre-delivery damage provisions
  • Date the receipt earlier to shift the risk
  • Treat signature alone as transfer of casualty risk

A signed instrument held to order differs from its release. Follow the specified trigger and damage procedure.

Lease desk

The operator pays two months’ cash deposit. The lease permits set-off by the lessor only, with replenishment on demand. Can the airline budget to skip the last two rents?

  • Only if it obtains a separate enforceable application right
  • Yes, because cash deposits always constitute prepaid rent
  • Yes, if no default was declared on signing

A lessor’s right to draw collateral is different from a lessee’s right to apply it as rent.

The forecast shop visit costs $1.2m. This lease reimburses eligible costs up to the engine bucket, which holds $700,000. The entire invoice is eligible. What is the airline’s unfunded bill in dollars?

$1.2m less the $700,000 reimbursable balance leaves $500,000. Payment timing can create an additional funding need.

At return, an engine must have 4,000 cycles remaining. It has 3,300. An agreed cash alternative charges $95 per missing cycle. No other adjustment applies. What amount in dollars settles this shortfall?

(4,000 − 3,300) × $95 = $66,500.

A parent has signed the lessor’s quiet enjoyment covenant. A separate senior mortgagee can enforce against the aircraft. What protection should the operator seek?

  • A binding non-disturbance undertaking from the creditor, with conditions the airline can satisfy
  • Another copy of the lease signed by the airline
  • Higher liability cover for passengers

Identify the party able to interrupt possession. A promise from another entity may leave the risk in place.

The airline cures late rent in the contractual grace period. The proposed enforcement memo still recommends taking possession immediately on that missed due date. What must be checked?

  • Whether a continuing event of default and the remedy’s procedural and local-law requirements exist
  • Only whether rent was ever late
  • Whether the owner has a spare set of keys

Breach, event of default, election and lawful enforcement are separate steps.

An export credit agency covers part of a lender’s losses. The airline wants to omit debt service from its downside forecast. What should its CFO do?

  • Keep the debt or lease obligations in the forecast and trace any recourse after payment
  • Reduce each instalment by the guarantee percentage
  • Treat agency support as a cash grant to the airline

A lender’s protection does not by itself discharge the airline’s payment obligations.

Cross-border closing

The authorised party has a recorded IDERA. A higher-ranking interest has not been discharged or consented. What assumption should be removed from the recovery model?

  • The need to identify the airframe
  • The need to budget for ferry costs

Check priority and required consents together with public-law conditions. A recorded authority is one part of the route.

An invoice combines aircraft supply, training and spare parts. A Dubai customer asserts the aircraft qualifies for zero rating. What should the signing note require?

  • Apply a single tax outcome solely because the invoice mentions an aircraft
  • Choose the lessor’s home rate for every line

Classification can depend on how items are supplied and where. Obtain a transaction-specific analysis.

Investment committee

A synthetic comparable set has P25 = 4, median = 6 and P75 = 10 business days. More days favour the airline. The clipped leverage score is +1. The opening range is 5 to 9 days. Predict the model landing in days.

6 + 0.5 × (10 − 6) = 8, inside the openings.

A single intermediary supplied three leases on identical paper. How should their matching redelivery clauses affect confidence?

  • Disclose their common drafting origin and test sensitivity after grouping them
  • Treat three identical clauses as independent market votes
  • Delete every disclosed return condition

Keeping the observed text and testing dependence is more informative than an unqualified observation count.

The client values the asset at $5.5m. Present transaction costs are $150,000. There is a 20% chance of $800,000 loss with $300,000 collectible in that state. No other risk or veto applies. What is the break-even base price in dollars?

Expected kept loss is $100,000. $5.5m − $150,000 − $100,000 = $5.25m.

The last draft before closing

Use this fictional packet for the next two items. The purchase agreement permits conditional technical acceptance. Its release schedule requires a completed discrepancy list signed by both parties. The escrow mandate permits payment only on their joint instruction. An email from the seller says the remaining dent can be dealt with after closing.

The buyer has signed the technical election. The seller’s engineer has marked the repair plan “reviewed”. The list has only the buyer’s signature. Which instruction fits this file?

  • Release because the engineer reviewed the plan
  • Hold the funds, obtain both authorised signatures or an effective amendment, and then satisfy the joint release mandate
  • Release because the seller sent the latest email

Technical agreement about the work, signing the list and authorising the wire are separate acts. The email alone supplies neither missing signature.

Assume the parties instead sign a cure escrow for $46,000. Eligible repair costs come to $63,000. The escrow pays on time and the buyer bears all excess. How much of the repair bill must the buyer fund itself, in dollars?

$63,000 less $46,000 leaves $17,000. This is the realised shortfall in the specified state.

In a separate fictional lease, the operator asks to repeat a financial representation only on delivery. A reviewer adds “no ongoing obligation after delivery” in the margin. The signed clause says the representation survives delivery and is made again on delivery using the latest audited accounts. No periodic repetition provision appears elsewhere.

Which status belongs in the issues list?

  • Survival means the representation is made anew every rent day
  • The margin note prevents a later claim for an inaccurate statement at delivery
  • Test the latest accounts at delivery; the statement survives, with no stated rent-date repetition

The dates on which facts are asserted, the evidence used at each date and the survival of rights are distinct controls.

A separate warranty schedule gives the owner conduct of a supplier claim. The operator receives collected proceeds up to its repair spend. What should the operator seek if collecting after handback is important?

  • Assume running the claim and keeping all proceeds are the same right
  • A surviving duty to pursue and account, a remittance deadline and access to the claim evidence
  • Declare the supplier bound by the operator’s internal claim log

The operator needs enforceable cooperation and a path from collection to payment. Check separately that the supplier’s own instrument permits the claim.

Operational control

In a fictional lease, an airline may provide flights to another carrier without a prior consent only while its own crew operates the aircraft under its continuing operational control. Transfer of possession or control otherwise requires prior written consent. The airline promises a customer operating rights for a weekend. Its pilots will fly, but the customer alone will direct the operation. No consent has arrived.

What should the airline do before releasing the aircraft for this operation?

  • Release because the airline supplies the pilots
  • Release if the customer promises to reimburse all claims
  • Hold the release until the arrangement satisfies the operational-control exception or the required consent is obtained, and check regulatory clearance separately

Supplying the crew satisfies only one part of this permission. Customer indemnity does not alter the possession covenant or supply regulatory authority.
