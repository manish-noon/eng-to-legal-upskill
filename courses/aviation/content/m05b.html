Reading the closing set

The shop says the aircraft is ready. The seller wants its money today. You can read the documents behind those two statements, tell the closing team which instruction it can give, and explain what would change your answer.

Find the paper that makes the decision

Start with a question precise enough to settle on the call. “Can we buy this aircraft?” sends everyone back through the whole file. “Can our authorized signatory release the price at noon while the repair invoice is still open?” tells you which certificate, payment condition and instruction to find. Put the executed purchase agreement, incorporated schedules, signed amendments and agreed escrow instructions beside one another. Keep the draft that prompted the negotiation available. You need it to explain what moved.

Read each document for its job. A specification describes the asset. A shop report records what someone observed. A signed election records which contractual option someone exercised. A release instruction directs a custodian to act. A page bearing a signature can serve any of those purposes. Check the parties, version, date, signature authority and referenced attachments. If two operative documents disagree, read any express priority rule and its scope. Get the conflict resolved before issuing instructions that assume an answer.

Consider an invented sale packet. Draft 4 made a buyer's technical acceptance unconditional. A margin comment asks for acceptance subject to repair. Executed §6 permits a conditional election only for items listed in the signed defect schedule. The attached form still contains an unconditional box. The buyer plans to check that box and staple on the shop report. You have found a problem: the contract specifies a signed list, and the proposed form says something different. Prepare a consistent election and get the signatures the executed contract requires before the election expires.

A buyer requested a seller guarantee in a comment. The executed schedule requires only an introduction to the service provider. Which obligation can you put on the closing checklist from this material?

  • The guarantee, because it explains the purpose of the introduction.
  • The introduction. Record the guarantee as a requested protection that you could not trace into the signed set.
  • Both, provided the buyer circulated the comment before signature.

The executed obligation supports asking for the introduction. The comment documents the request. Its timing supplies no promise from the seller.

The closing folder has two different copies marked “final”. What would settle which attachment was signed?

Retrieve the completed signing package or counterpart set, match the exhibit reference and compare the actual attached pages. A file modification timestamp tells you when someone saved a copy.

Follow one election through a public filing

Open the filed Global 5000 purchase agreement used in module 05. Start at the definition of Discrepancies. It sends you to the delivery condition in Exhibit B and identifies the inspection facility as the decision maker on noncompliance. Next find §3.4. Completion of inspection and delivery of the final written report to both parties start the two-business-day election window. The purchaser sends an executed Exhibit C to the seller, copying escrow. It can accept, accept subject to correction, or reject. Section 3.5 supplies the result of silence. These steps tell you both what qualifies and how the purchaser exercises its choice. Filed agreement, definitions and §§3.4–3.5.

Follow conditional acceptance forward. Under §3.6 the seller pays to correct qualifying findings. The facility can require a verification flight. The purchaser bears specified flight costs, so a seller-funded repair can still draw on the buyer's budget. At §4.3.7 the applicable airframe and engine maintenance contracts listed in Schedule 1 to Exhibit B must be current at closing; transfer depends on the provider permitting it. A shop's repair sign-off cannot establish whether a provider permits transfer. Ask for the provider evidence separately. Filed agreement, §§3.6 and 4.3.7.

Now follow the money. The deposit definition ties its move to non-refundable status to acceptance evidenced by Exhibit C. Exhibit D serves as the delivery receipt. Read §§4.1 and 4.4 before equating an election with permission to pay the seller. Executed transfer papers can already sit undated with escrow. On the closing call the parties give linked instructions concerning releases, filings, registration and payment. Section 4.4 makes the events contingent on one another. The signed inspection election tells you why the buyer is still in the deal. The call instructions tell you when escrow can complete it. Filed agreement, deposit definition, §§4.1 and 4.4, Exhibits C and D.

In the filed agreement the seller has deposited signed transfer papers. The purchaser sends Exhibit C accepting subject to repairs. What should the closing coordinator do next?

  • Release the papers immediately because escrow already has them.
  • Track §3.6 corrections, then verify closing conditions and obtain the §4.4 instructions.
  • Send Exhibit D to the shop as authority to start repairs.

The election leads to correction. Custody of signed papers lets escrow act later. Exhibit D serves a different event.

Run the competing clocks

The rest of this lesson uses a separate, invented closing set. All times are Dubai time. Business days for this exercise are Monday through Friday, with no holidays. Executed §6 requires a signed election by 16:00 on the second business day after both parties receive the final report. Silence means rejection. Section 9 allows termination by written notice after Friday at 18:00 if closing has not occurred. Section 12 requires notices to reach two specified mailboxes. The escrow instruction separately sets Friday 14:00 as the bank cutoff for same-day payment.

The buyer gets the final report on Tuesday. The seller gets it Wednesday. Count Thursday and Friday. Friday at 16:00 is the election deadline, but treasury loses the same-day payment route two hours earlier. Friday at 18:00 opens a termination right. It does not send the notice. Put an owner and a receipt beside each deadline. Ask treasury whether it can position funds early without authorizing release. Ask counsel for a signed extension while the parties still have time to deliver it.

Both sides received the report as described. At Friday 14:05 the bank cutoff has passed. No election has been delivered. Which statement fits the file?

  • The sale terminated five minutes ago.
  • The election deadline moves to the next banking day.
  • The buyer still has until 16:00 to deliver its election. Same-day payment needs a separate solution.

The bank cutoff controls payment logistics. The signed election and termination clauses have separate triggers.

The broker replies “extension fine” to a group chat. Which clocks can you erase?

Keep all three visible. Check authority and amendment formalities, secure the required signatures and confirm delivery. Even a valid extension between principals cannot extend a bank's processing hours.

Convert the report into a decision

In this fictional inspection the cargo door passes its functional check. The installation record supplied for its actuator carries a different serial number from the fitted unit. The delivery list requires a matching record. The shop also notes a scrape on a removable cargo divider and confirms it falls within the agreed wear limit. The buyer can require the promised actuator record and can ask the seller for a cosmetic allowance. Only the first request enforces an existing delivery condition.

Make the signed exception schedule usable by someone who never attended the inspection. Give each item an identifier, its contract reference, the observed fact, the required evidence, who supplies it, who verifies it and the latest time for action. A video of the door opening shows what it did during the check. It cannot identify the actuator covered by the installation record. Ask the shop to reconcile the serial numbers and supply the matching record, or obtain an alternative expressly accepted by everyone whose consent matters. If a lender requires the original record before funding, the buyer's willingness to accept a copy will not fund this closing.

Invented election: Buyer accepts subject to item R on the attached signed schedule. All other closing conditions continue to apply.

Read this against the executed §6 in the example. Check the schedule is attached, signed and complete when delivered. If item R never appears there, a later forwarded shop email may arrive outside the election window. Have a named recipient acknowledge delivery of the exact package and save that acknowledgment with the copy sent.

The executed contract requires a signed exception schedule. An authorized buyer signs the election but attaches yesterday's list, which omits the record gap. Thirty minutes remain. What should you prioritize?

  • Ask the shop to add the gap to tomorrow's invoice.
  • Check the replacement-election procedure and deliver a correctly signed, complete package within the window.
  • Assume the seller knows about the gap because it attended inspection.

Knowledge of the defect does not supply the required election. You still have time to use the applicable delivery process or secure a written agreement.

The buyer accepts a replacement record. Its lender insists on an original before releasing funds. What must happen before this financed closing can proceed?

  • Escrow can declare the replacement authentic enough.
  • The shop can initial the exception schedule for the lender.
  • Supply the required original or obtain the lender's authorized change to its funding condition.

The lender controls its advance. Buyer consent changes only what the buyer is entitled to accept.

Trade time, cash and proof

Suppose a different, fully documented cabin repair can legally wait until after delivery. The lender agrees. The seller wants sale proceeds this week and fears an open-ended repair claim. The buyer wants enough cash to finish the work and a remedy if the shop discovers more damage. A price credit reduces what the buyer pays and can leave it bearing the overrun. A holdback keeps part of the seller's proceeds in escrow. Write down who ultimately gets that cash, what proves entitlement, how disputes are handled and who pays when the reserve runs out.

Price the concession before recommending it. In this exercise the fixed shop quote is AED 84,000, necessary positioning costs AED 12,000 and three days of substitute transport cost AED 6,000 each. These three costs do not overlap. The seller offers a credit of AED 95,000 as a full settlement. Compare that with the cash the buyer expects to spend. Then ask whether the quote covers a failed verification test and who carries any increase in downtime. Give your principal both the quantified base case and the open issue.

How many AED of the estimated base cost remain with the buyer after the offered credit?

84,000 + 12,000 + 3 × 6,000 = 114,000. Subtract the 95,000 credit to leave AED 19,000.

Instead the parties reserve AED 160,000 from seller proceeds. Accepted invoices of AED 94,000 are paid from it. At expiry the instructions retain AED 28,000 for a disputed invoice. How many AED can escrow return to the seller now?

160,000 less 94,000 paid and 28,000 still disputed leaves AED 38,000 for release. The disputed amount stays in escrow pending the agreed decision.

Would doubling the reserve solve the missing-original problem from the previous section?

Only if the funding condition is changed or satisfied. A larger reserve makes more money available for a covered claim after closing. The lender's condition determines whether there will be a funded closing at all.

Give an instruction someone can execute

Give the call leader three separate answers. What has the shop verified? Which conditions can the buyer confirm, and who else must consent? What can escrow release right now? The diagram follows a closing where the shop has finished its work but the lender has yet to confirm funding. Keep each answer next to its evidence and the name of the person entitled to act.

For a hold, name the missing item, owner, recipient, deadline and consequence if it never arrives. For a close, identify the instruction version, amounts, payees and linked document releases. For termination, check the right has arisen, who may exercise it, the prescribed route and the deposit accounting. A missed closing can give one party an option it prefers to keep open. Calling the deal dead too early can deprive your team of the chance to negotiate a short extension.

Write the hold instruction in language the person holding the money can use. “Retain the full balance pending joint release instructions. The buyer’s finance lead will circulate written lender clearance to both principals and escrow by 13:30 Dubai time. If clearance is missing, keep the funds in place and reconvene before the 14:00 cutoff.” Put a separate line beside the trustee or filing agent if it holds signed papers. Confirm receipt with each custodian. When the evidence arrives, compare the clearance against the condition it was supposed to satisfy. A lender acknowledging an email may still be reviewing the attachment.

On the Dubai call, show the contractual time zone beside the local time. Have the filing agent and bank confirm their own cutoffs for the actual date. Do not transplant the exercise calendar into your live file.

Friday at 18:05, the sale remains open under the invented §9. The buyer wants to wait for the lender on Monday. What can you tell it?

  • Waiting guarantees the seller must close on Monday.
  • Check who may terminate now, whether notice has arrived, and secure a signed extension if the buyer needs certainty.
  • Send a termination notice to preserve the deal until Monday.

The termination right creates an option. If the seller can exercise it, the buyer cannot assure a Monday closing by waiting.

All repair evidence is in. Escrow's signed instruction pays the full balance to the seller, but tonight's signed buyer–seller amendment requires a holdback. Escrow must assent to changed instructions. What belongs on the call sheet?

  • Release in full and recover the reserve later.
  • Tell escrow the amendment automatically changes its mandate.
  • Hold disbursement until escrow and the required parties adopt matching instructions, then verify the revised split.

The principals agreed the economics. Escrow still needs the authority required by its mandate to implement the payment split.

Key terms

Executed set
The signed documents and attachments that record this transaction.
Priority rule
A provision specifying which text controls a conflict within its stated scope.
Election window
The period for exercising a contractual choice in the prescribed way.
Exception schedule
A list of identified matters reserved from an otherwise broader acceptance.
Verification flight
A flight used to check whether specified corrections worked.
Bank cutoff
The latest time a bank accepts an instruction for a particular processing date.
Funding condition
A requirement the financier sets for advancing money.
Disputed sum
An amount whose entitlement remains unresolved under the agreed procedure.
Release authority
The permission the holder of funds or documents needs to deliver them.
