Aircraft agreements / Decision reference

Before you clear the aircraft deal

One aircraft, several clocks. Trace the paper to the cash, the creditor and the person who can release each condition.

Read the exchange

Map each promise

Identify the actor, object and serial number, trigger, deadline, exception, remedy and surviving claim. Follow schedules and side letters.

Buying

Rebuild specification, escalation, credits and deposit ledger. Align inspection, insurance, payment, lien releases and delivery of the bill of sale.

Leasing

Separate rent, refundable collateral and condition-dependent reserves. Price missing life and records. Check the competing creditor’s undertaking.

Predict the landing

Compare the same clock

Filter by deal family, remedy, unit and starting event. Keep redacted amounts unknown. Record sample size, common forms and the source rows.

Score the client’s position

Who has the pen? Who can replace the slot, aircraft or financing in time? Score distinct facts −1, 0 or +1 and clip the sum to ±2.

Median + |score| / 2 × (selected edge − median)

Choose the quartile that favours the stronger side. Bound the result by the opening asks. With thin evidence, state a conditional range.

Price the decision

Kept = p × max(0, loss − collectible recovery)

Convert to one currency and date. Include timing, deductibles, exclusions, recourse and credit usability. Sum distinct states. Report tail and peak cash separately.

Break-even base price

Value ceiling less other costs and expected kept loss. A deposit credited at closing is counted once.

Sign

Within the ceiling, tail funded and all mandate gates clear.

Negotiate

Name a feasible concession, its minimum cash value, owner and deadline. Make signing conditional on the revised paper.

Walk

An unbridgeable price gap or an uncured hard stop. State what would reopen the decision.

Cross-border release

Verify national and international searches, senior consents, applicable declarations, export evidence, tax and forum with the advisers responsible.
